THE LAST GLAZIER is a stained-glass restoration game. You are the last glazier of a hill chapel, and the night is coming alive to eat the light: restore three great windows before the final bell. Two artisan gestures drive play. FIT: drag jewel-glass shards from the workbench into their ghost outlines in the window, rotating them with Q/E, the mouse wheel or right-click — a first-try placement is a PERFECT FIT and builds your streak. SEAL: once every shard of a circuit is placed, its lead seam glows gold; hold the pointer down and trace the molten solder tip along the seam to seal the circuit, igniting the whole region and throwing coloured light across the chapel floor. All the while the Darkness creeps in from the edges — wrong drops feed it, sealed circuits push it back, and if it fills before the window is whole, NIGHT FALLS. Seal every circuit and DAWN BREAKS. Vanilla JavaScript and Canvas 2D, WebAudio-synthesized sound (glass chimes, solder sizzle, the chapel bell), no build step and no external assets.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. To produce the hand-in output instead, run `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts` from the repository root.
